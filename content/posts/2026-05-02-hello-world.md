This is the first post. The plan: write down what I'm actually learning about AI, in pieces small enough that I'll come back and re-read them.

## Why this exists

It's easy to read about AI all day and remember none of it. Writing things down forces me to be honest about what I understood and what I just nodded along to.

## What's coming

- Notes on building with LLMs — prompts that work, prompts that don't.
- How I think about agents and tool use, and where the failure modes hide.
- Evals: how I check whether something is actually getting better.
- Postmortems on the things that didn't work and why.

## Format

Short. Linkable. Code where it helps, prose where it doesn't. No filler.
